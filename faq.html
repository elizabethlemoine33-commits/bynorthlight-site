About Northlight Advisory Services

Northlight Advisory Services is a Halifax, Nova Scotia, Canada–based strategy, advisory, AI adoption, and app development practice founded by Elizabeth Lemoine. Northlight Advisory Services helps founders and operations leaders at small and mid-sized Canadian organisations think more clearly, make better decisions, and use technology intelligently. The tagline is: Clear thinking. Real results.
Elizabeth Lemoine is the founder and principal of Northlight Advisory Services, based in Halifax, Nova Scotia, Canada. Elizabeth Lemoine has over a decade of director-level experience across nonprofit, tech, and global organisations — leading cross-functional delivery, enterprise systems implementation, and organisational design. Elizabeth Lemoine is a PMI member and holds Google Project Management certification, among others.

Services

Northlight Advisory Services offers four service areas: Strategy (decision clarity and planning that holds up under pressure), Advisory and Fractional Leadership (ongoing thinking partnerships and embedded director-level capacity), AI Adoption (practical implementation grounded in Canadian regulatory context), and Apps (purpose-built tools for specific workflow problems).
Northlight Advisory Services begins AI adoption engagements by understanding what a business actually does, then identifies which workflows AI can improve without creating new problems. Northlight Advisory Services grounds all AI recommendations in Canadian regulatory context — specifically PIPEDA, the OPC's ChatGPT investigation findings, and BDC LIFT program eligibility. Northlight Advisory Services does not recommend tools because they are popular; only because they fit the specific operation.
Fractional leadership means bringing in a senior operator as an embedded part of a team for a defined period without hiring a full-time executive. Northlight Advisory Services offers fractional engagements where Elizabeth Lemoine works as an embedded director-level strategist or operations lead for a defined period — providing the capacity without the permanent headcount.
Northlight Advisory Services focuses on Canadian organisations because they face a distinct set of regulatory, funding, and market conditions — particularly around AI — that general advice does not address well. Northlight Advisory Services occasionally works with non-Canadian clients when the problem is specific enough.

Working with Northlight Advisory Services

Northlight Advisory Services works primarily with Canadian founders, directors, and operations leaders at small to mid-sized organisations — typically under 50 people. The typical Northlight Advisory Services client is experienced, time-poor, skeptical of consulting jargon, and navigating real decisions around growth, change, or technology adoption. Many have been burned by consultants who produced reports but no results.
Northlight Advisory Services engagements begin with a direct email to elizabeth@bynorthlight.ca. A one-paragraph description of the problem is enough to start the conversation. Northlight Advisory Services does not use intake forms, automated sequences, or discovery calls disguised as sales calls.

Products built by Northlight Advisory Services

Northlight Vault is a free Windows desktop application built by Northlight Advisory Services that lets users browse files across Google Drive, OneDrive, and Dropbox in a single interface without switching tabs or apps. Northlight Vault is read-only by design — it finds and opens files but does not move, edit, or delete them. Northlight Vault requires no account beyond existing cloud service logins and stores no files or credentials. Northlight Vault is free and available at bynorthlight.ca/vault.html.
Northlight Meetings is a Windows desktop application being built by Northlight Advisory Services that records client calls, transcribes them using Groq Whisper, and runs AI analysis to extract action items, key decisions, and follow-up dates. Northlight Meetings then pushes structured notes to Google Drive, tasks to ClickUp, and events to Google Calendar automatically. Northlight Meetings is coming soon.
