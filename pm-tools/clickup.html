Why this tool?
ClickUp is built for teams running multiple workstreams with operational depth. The layered Spaces/Folders/Lists hierarchy and powerful automation engine make it a strong fit for advisory and operations-heavy teams. It scales from a solo practice to a larger team without forcing a platform switch — and the native MCP connector is the strongest integration story in this group.
Key Capabilities
  • Views: List, Board, Calendar, Gantt, Timeline, Mind Map, Workload
  • Subtasks: Yes, with unlimited nesting on paid plans
  • Dependencies: Yes — with lag times and blocking logic
  • Automations: 100/mo (Free) · 1,000/mo (Unlimited) · 5,000+/mo (Business+)
  • Dashboards: Custom dashboards (Business+ plans)
  • Guest access: Free guests all plans; permission-controlled on paid
  • Time tracking: Built in on all paid plans
  • Mobile: iOS and Android
