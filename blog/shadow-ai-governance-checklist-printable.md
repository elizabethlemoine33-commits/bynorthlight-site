<h1 class="doc-title">Shadow AI Governance Checklist</h1>
<p class="doc-subtitle">Four questions to run before you write a policy — you can do this in an afternoon</p>

<div class="step">
  <div class="step-header">
    <span class="step-number">Question 1</span>
    <span class="step-title">What AI tools are people actually using?</span>
  </div>
  <div class="step-rule"></div>
  <ul class="checklist">
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>Ask each team directly — not "what's approved," but what are they actually running today</li>
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>Include personal accounts (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini) used for work tasks</li>
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>Include tools embedded in other software (Copilot in Word, AI features in your CRM)</li>
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>Record every tool named — don't filter or judge at this stage</li>
  </ul>
</div>

<div class="step">
  <div class="step-header">
    <span class="step-number">Question 2</span>
    <span class="step-title">What data are they putting into those tools?</span>
  </div>
  <div class="step-rule"></div>
  <ul class="checklist">
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>For each tool identified, ask: what kind of information goes in?</li>
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>Flag immediately: customer names, emails, financial data, employee records, contract terms, strategy documents</li>
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>Confirm whether personal information (as defined under PIPEDA) is being processed</li>
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>Note whether data is going to tools with Canadian data residency or US-based servers</li>
  </ul>
</div>

<div class="step">
  <div class="step-header">
    <span class="step-number">Question 3</span>
    <span class="step-title">Who owns the decision about whether that's acceptable?</span>
  </div>
  <div class="step-rule"></div>
  <ul class="checklist">
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>Name one person — not a committee — who can say yes or no on each tool and data type</li>
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>Confirm that person has the information they need to make that call</li>
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>If no one currently owns this decision, name who should — and get their agreement today</li>
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>Document the decision owner by tool category (not one-off, per tool)</li>
  </ul>
</div>

<div class="step">
  <div class="step-header">
    <span class="step-number">Question 4</span>
    <span class="step-title">What is the one next decision we need to make?</span>
  </div>
  <div class="step-rule"></div>
  <ul class="checklist">
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>Based on what you found: which tool or data type is the highest-risk item?</li>
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>Make one concrete decision on that item before this meeting ends (approve it, restrict it, or escalate it)</li>
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>Set a date to revisit the rest — don't let the full list become a parking lot</li>
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>Communicate what was decided and why to the people affected — don't govern silently</li>
  </ul>
</div>

<div class="step">
  <div class="step-header">
    <span class="step-number">After</span>
    <span class="step-title">What to do with what you find</span>
  </div>
  <div class="step-rule"></div>
  <ul class="checklist">
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>Tools with no personal data and no compliance exposure: document them as approved</li>
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>Tools processing personal data: review vendor data processing terms and update your privacy policy</li>
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>Tools you can't get clarity on: restrict use until the decision owner has reviewed</li>
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>Next step: write a one-page acceptable use policy so people know what's allowed going forward</li>
  </ul>
</div>
