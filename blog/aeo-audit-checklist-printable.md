<h1 class="doc-title">AEO Audit Checklist</h1>
<p class="doc-subtitle">Six areas to check before AI engines decide whether to cite you</p>

<div class="step">
  <div class="step-header">
    <span class="step-number">Area 1</span>
    <span class="step-title">FAQ sections — visible and in schema</span>
  </div>
  <div class="step-rule"></div>
  <ul class="checklist">
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>Every post on a topic people ask questions about has a visible FAQ section — not just a schema block buried in JSON</li>
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>FAQPage schema (JSON-LD) is present on pages with FAQ sections, with each question and full answer included</li>
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>FAQ questions are phrased the way people actually ask them — not internal jargon ("What is a fractional COO?", not "Fractional leadership overview")</li>
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>Each FAQ answer is self-contained — a reader who only sees that answer has everything they need</li>
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>At least 5 FAQ questions per post on complex topics; at least 3 on shorter pieces</li>
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>FAQ section is in the HTML body and visible to users, not hidden or collapsed by default</li>
  </ul>
</div>

<div class="step">
  <div class="step-header">
    <span class="step-number">Area 2</span>
    <span class="step-title">Answer blocks — lead with the answer</span>
  </div>
  <div class="step-rule"></div>
  <ul class="checklist">
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>Key sections start with a direct answer in the first 1–2 sentences before expanding with detail</li>
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>Definition questions ("What is X?") are answered in 40–60 words that could stand alone as a citation</li>
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>Comparison questions ("X vs. Y") include a direct verdict, not just a list of differences</li>
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>Avoid burying the answer after long preambles — AI skims for extractable answers; so does your reader</li>
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>Count your answer blocks: aim for at least 3 per post on substantive topics</li>
  </ul>
</div>

<div class="step">
  <div class="step-header">
    <span class="step-number">Area 3</span>
    <span class="step-title">Numbered sequences — use &lt;ol&gt; for ordered content</span>
  </div>
  <div class="step-rule"></div>
  <ul class="checklist">
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>Step-by-step processes use &lt;ol&gt; (ordered list), not bullet points — order carries information AI engines use for structured extraction</li>
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>Numbered sequences have at least 3 items and are written to be understood out of context</li>
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>HowTo schema is present on pages that explain a process, with each step as a structured object</li>
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>Steps are specific, not vague — "Choose one KPI to track this quarter" not "Set goals"</li>
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>Review your site's total &lt;ol&gt; count — fewer than 1 per page is a citability gap worth addressing</li>
  </ul>
</div>

<div class="step">
  <div class="step-header">
    <span class="step-number">Area 4</span>
    <span class="step-title">Comparison content — tables and side-by-sides</span>
  </div>
  <div class="step-rule"></div>
  <ul class="checklist">
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>Comparison posts include at least one HTML &lt;table&gt; with clear column headers</li>
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>Tables have a verdict row or summary — the comparison leads to a conclusion, not just a list</li>
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>Table content is visible in the HTML, not rendered via JavaScript from an external source</li>
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>At least one comparison page exists for your most-searched "X vs. Y" topic in your domain</li>
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>Tables have a caption or introductory sentence that explains what's being compared</li>
  </ul>
</div>

<div class="step">
  <div class="step-header">
    <span class="step-number">Area 5</span>
    <span class="step-title">Technical signals — structured data and crawl access</span>
  </div>
  <div class="step-rule"></div>
  <ul class="checklist">
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>Article or BlogPosting schema is present on every blog post, with headline, datePublished, author, and publisher</li>
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>Organization schema is present on the homepage with name, url, and sameAs links to your social profiles</li>
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>sitemap.xml is accessible and up to date — includes all published pages and posts</li>
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>robots.txt is not blocking AI crawlers (GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended)</li>
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>llms.txt is present at /llms.txt — lists your key pages and their purpose in plain language</li>
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>All canonical URLs are correct and match the live URL — no canonical pointing to a 404</li>
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>Page load time is under 3 seconds — slow pages are less likely to be crawled fully</li>
  </ul>
</div>

<div class="step">
  <div class="step-header">
    <span class="step-number">Area 6</span>
    <span class="step-title">Authority signals — E-E-A-T and citation readiness</span>
  </div>
  <div class="step-rule"></div>
  <ul class="checklist">
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>Author bio is present on blog posts — first-hand experience is stated, not implied</li>
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>Claims are grounded in experience or cited sources — vague assertions don't get cited</li>
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>At least one post on your highest-traffic topic includes a link to a primary source (research, official publication, or industry body)</li>
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>Your business entity (full legal name) is consistent across the website, Google Business Profile, LinkedIn, and schema</li>
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>Contact information is findable and crawlable — a visible email address or form, not hidden behind obfuscation</li>
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>Run an AI visibility tool (MentionScout, Profound, or similar) to see how often you're being cited — baseline before optimizing</li>
  </ul>
</div>
