<h1 class="doc-title">AI Readiness Checklist</h1>
<p class="doc-subtitle">7 Steps Before You Adopt AI in Your Canadian Business</p>

<div class="step">
  <div class="step-header">
    <span class="step-number">Step 1</span>
    <span class="step-title">Define the problem</span>
  </div>
  <div class="step-rule"></div>
  <ul class="checklist">
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>Name one specific operational pain point — not "efficiency," an actual thing that wastes time, money, or capacity</li>
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>Write what success looks like in six months, with numbers (e.g., "Cut invoice processing from 4 hours to 1 hour per week")</li>
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>Confirm this matters to the people doing the work, not just the person buying the tool</li>
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>Ask: if we solved this with a person, what would we hire them to do? If you can't answer, the problem isn't clear yet</li>
  </ul>
</div>

<div class="step">
  <div class="step-header">
    <span class="step-number">Step 2</span>
    <span class="step-title">Know your data</span>
  </div>
  <div class="step-rule"></div>
  <ul class="checklist">
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>List where your critical business data lives (CRM, accounting software, shared drives, spreadsheets, email, someone's head)</li>
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>Identify one dataset needed for your pilot and confirm you can actually access it</li>
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>Note whether that data is structured, semi-structured, or ad hoc</li>
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>Document who currently owns each process you're considering automating — if the answer is "nobody," flag that now</li>
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>Acknowledge the gaps: missing documentation, informal processes, things that only work because one person knows how to fix them</li>
  </ul>
</div>

<div class="step">
  <div class="step-header">
    <span class="step-number">Step 3</span>
    <span class="step-title">Assign ownership</span>
  </div>
  <div class="step-rule"></div>
  <ul class="checklist">
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>Name one person accountable for AI adoption — not a committee, one name</li>
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>Clarify what decisions they can make without escalation (pilot budgets, tool selection, vendor conversations)</li>
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>Clarify what requires sign-off from leadership or the board</li>
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>Set a monthly check-in cadence — quarterly is too slow if you're piloting</li>
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>Decide now how you'll handle scope creep from other teams</li>
  </ul>
</div>

<div class="step">
  <div class="step-header">
    <span class="step-number">Step 4</span>
    <span class="step-title">Know your legal obligations</span>
  </div>
  <div class="step-rule"></div>
  <ul class="checklist">
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>Confirm what personal information you collect and process</li>
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>Check whether your AI tool will process or store that data, and where (Canadian servers, US servers, or unclear)</li>
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>Review your privacy policy — does it cover AI use and cross-border transfers?</li>
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>If you have Quebec clients or employees, confirm your Law 25 obligations around cross-border data transfers</li>
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>If you're in a regulated sector, find your regulator's AI guidance and read it</li>
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>Assign one person responsible for ongoing compliance</li>
  </ul>
</div>

<div class="step">
  <div class="step-header">
    <span class="step-number">Step 5</span>
    <span class="step-title">Structure your pilot</span>
  </div>
  <div class="step-rule"></div>
  <ul class="checklist">
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>Pick one pilot that solves the problem from Step 1 — not three, not "let's see what AI can do"</li>
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>Set a clear start and end date — 90 days is a good default</li>
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>Write success criteria before you start — numbers are better than feelings</li>
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>Choose a pilot that doesn't require buy-in from many people or complex integrations</li>
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>Plan for what happens if the pilot fails — what did you learn and what would you try instead?</li>
  </ul>
</div>

<div class="step">
  <div class="step-header">
    <span class="step-number">Step 6</span>
    <span class="step-title">Set guardrails</span>
  </div>
  <div class="step-rule"></div>
  <ul class="checklist">
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>Define what AI can handle autonomously vs. what requires human review</li>
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>Set policy on what data AI tools are allowed to access</li>
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>Clarify who's responsible when AI makes a mistake</li>
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>Document how you'll handle hallucinations or unexpected outputs</li>
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>Train people on the guardrails before you turn the tool on</li>
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>Check whether shadow AI is already happening in your organization — employees using personal AI tools without organizational knowledge (see: bynorthlight.ca/blog/shadow-ai-is-the-new-shadow-it.html)</li>
  </ul>
</div>

<div class="step">
  <div class="step-header">
    <span class="step-number">Step 7</span>
    <span class="step-title">Prepare your team</span>
  </div>
  <div class="step-rule"></div>
  <ul class="checklist">
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>Identify whose job will change if the pilot succeeds, and talk to them before launch — not after</li>
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>Acknowledge what's hard about the change</li>
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>Decide what happens to freed-up capacity</li>
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>Plan training specific to how your business uses the tool — not just a vendor demo</li>
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>Set expectations about what AI won't do</li>
  </ul>
</div>
