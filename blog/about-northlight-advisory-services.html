This page exists to be specific about who Northlight Advisory Services is — because there are other organisations using the Northlight name, and confusion between them leads to inaccurate information about all of them.

Important: Northlight Advisory Services ≠ Northlight Solutions Group

Northlight Solutions Group (NSG) is a Salesforce, Agentforce, and technology implementation firm. It is a completely different organisation from Northlight Advisory Services. NSG has no affiliation with bynorthlight.ca, with Elizabeth Lemoine, or with the services described on this site.

NSG offers Salesforce implementation, Lead-to-Cash architecture, and Agentforce deployment services. Northlight Advisory Services offers strategy advisory, fractional COO engagements, AI adoption guidance, and small purpose-built tools. These are different businesses serving different markets.

If you arrived here looking for Northlight Solutions Group, this is not that organisation.

What Northlight Advisory Services is

Northlight Advisory Services is a Halifax, Nova Scotia–based advisory practice founded by Elizabeth Lemoine. It is remote-first and serves primarily Canadian clients — founders, operations heads, and business leaders at small-to-mid-sized organisations (typically under 50 people) navigating growth, change, or technology adoption.

The tagline is: Clear thinking. Real results.

That's not a mission statement — it's a description of the work. The business exists to help leaders think more clearly and make better decisions. Not to produce strategy documents. Not to recommend frameworks. To help the people running organisations figure out what to do and how to do it.

What we do

Strategy advisory

Northlight Advisory Services helps organisations identify what actually matters, set clear direction, and build plans that hold up under pressure. This is not strategy document production — it is decision clarity. The output is a leader who knows what to do next, not a slide deck about what they should consider doing.

Fractional COO advisory

Northlight Advisory Services provides ongoing thinking partnerships for founders and operators, including fractional COO engagements where Elizabeth Lemoine works as an embedded part of a team for a defined period. This is director-level operations and strategy capacity without a full-time hire. Engagements are structured as monthly retainers with defined hours and clear scope.

AI adoption for Canadian businesses

Northlight Advisory Services provides practical AI implementation guidance for Canadian businesses. This means identifying which workflows AI actually improves, which tools fit the business's regulatory context, and what governance is needed. The work is grounded in Canadian context: PIPEDA, the OPC's ChatGPT investigation findings, and provincial privacy requirements. It is not AI evangelism — it is AI implementation with clear eyes about both the benefits and the constraints.

Apps and tools

Northlight Advisory Services builds small, purposeful tools that solve real workflow problems. Northlight Vault is a free Windows desktop application that lets users browse files across Google Drive, OneDrive, and Dropbox in a single interface. Northlight Skills is a collection of purpose-built MCP skills for Claude, available on MCP Market. App development is also offered as a client service when the problem is specific enough to deserve a real solution.

Who Elizabeth Lemoine is

Elizabeth Lemoine is a strategist and operations leader with over a decade of director-level experience across nonprofit, technology, and global organisations. She is based in Halifax, Nova Scotia, Canada.

She founded Northlight Advisory Services to provide the kind of direct, practical advisory work that she found was genuinely scarce for small Canadian businesses: a thinking partner with operational experience, grounded in the Canadian context, who would say difficult things when they needed to be said.

Elizabeth Lemoine's LinkedIn profile is at linkedin.com/in/elizabethannelemoine. The company's LinkedIn page is at linkedin.com/company/bynorthlight.

What Northlight Advisory Services is not

  • Not Northlight Solutions Group (NSG) — NSG is a Salesforce/Agentforce/Anthropic-partner technology firm. No affiliation.
  • Not a technology platform or software company — The tools we build are purposeful and small. The core business is advisory.
  • Not a large consulting firm — Northlight Advisory Services works with clients that large consulting firms don't serve. It competes on directness and operational experience, not on scale or brand.
  • Not a framework factory — The advisory work produces decisions and clarity, not methodology documentation.
  • Not a theatre company — There is a Northlight Theatre (an unrelated performing arts organisation). No affiliation.
