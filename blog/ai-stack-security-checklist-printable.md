<h1 class="doc-title">AI Stack Security Checklist</h1>
<p class="doc-subtitle">Use when adding a new AI tool, during quarterly reviews, or when onboarding a new team member</p>

<div class="step">
  <div class="step-header">
    <span class="step-number">Part 1</span>
    <span class="step-title">Before You Connect a New Tool</span>
  </div>
  <div class="step-rule"></div>

  <p class="step-section-label">OAuth &amp; Access Scope</p>
  <ul class="checklist">
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>I have read the permissions screen — not just clicked through it</li>
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>The access requested makes sense for the feature I'm enabling</li>
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>If broader access is requested than seems necessary, I have asked the vendor to explain why</li>
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>I understand which apps this tool will connect to and what it will be able to read and modify</li>
  </ul>

  <p class="step-section-label">Data Handling</p>
  <ul class="checklist">
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>I know where this vendor stores data (Canada, US, EU — specify: ______)</li>
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>I know how long this vendor retains user data: ______</li>
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>I know whether my data will be used to train the model (yes / no / opt-out available)</li>
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>If customer or employee personal information will pass through this tool, I have read the vendor's data processing agreement or privacy policy</li>
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>If the vendor is US-based and we handle Canadian personal information, I have documented this in our data inventory and privacy policy</li>
  </ul>

  <p class="step-section-label">PIPEDA Compliance</p>
  <ul class="checklist">
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>Our privacy policy reflects the fact that this tool will process personal information</li>
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>We have a valid basis for disclosing personal data to this vendor (consent, contractual necessity, or other)</li>
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>If required, we have a data processing agreement in place with this vendor</li>
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>We have communicated to clients/contacts (where required) that their information may be processed by a third party in another country</li>
  </ul>

  <p class="step-section-label">Breach Response</p>
  <ul class="checklist">
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>I know the vendor's breach notification timeline: ______</li>
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>That timeline meets our PIPEDA reporting obligations (notification to the Privacy Commissioner must happen "as soon as feasible")</li>
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>Our internal incident response process covers this vendor</li>
  </ul>

  <p class="step-section-label">Access Control</p>
  <ul class="checklist">
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>The team members who will have access to this tool are documented</li>
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>Access levels (admin vs. user) are appropriate to each person's role</li>
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>We have a plan for revoking access when someone leaves or changes roles</li>
  </ul>
</div>

<div class="step">
  <div class="step-header">
    <span class="step-number">Part 2</span>
    <span class="step-title">Quarterly Review of Existing Tools</span>
  </div>
  <div class="step-rule"></div>
  <p style="font-size:13px;color:#666;margin-bottom:16px;">Run this for every AI tool currently in use. Approximately 15 minutes per tool.</p>

  <p class="step-section-label">Usage Audit</p>
  <ul class="checklist">
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>Tool is still actively used (if not, consider cancelling and revoking access)</li>
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>The use cases it was adopted for are still the primary use cases</li>
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>User list is current — no former employees, contractors, or partners with active access</li>
  </ul>

  <p class="step-section-label">Data Access Review</p>
  <ul class="checklist">
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>Authorized OAuth connections are still necessary and appropriate</li>
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>No connections exist to apps we no longer use or that have changed their data handling policies</li>
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>Data retention terms have not changed since we adopted the tool</li>
  </ul>

  <p class="step-section-label">Policy Alignment</p>
  <ul class="checklist">
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>This tool is listed in our approved tools policy</li>
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>The data handling rules for this tool are documented and team members know what they are</li>
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>Any customer-facing data processed by this tool is reflected in our current privacy policy</li>
  </ul>
</div>

<div class="step">
  <div class="step-header">
    <span class="step-number">Part 3</span>
    <span class="step-title">Team Member Onboarding</span>
  </div>
  <div class="step-rule"></div>

  <ul class="checklist">
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>New team member has received a copy of our AI tools policy</li>
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>New team member understands which tools are approved and for which purposes</li>
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>New team member has been briefed on what types of data cannot be entered into public AI tools</li>
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>New team member has been briefed on our incident response process — specifically, who to contact if they suspect a data issue</li>
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>Access has been granted only to the tools and data the role requires</li>
  </ul>
</div>

<div class="step">
  <div class="step-header">
    <span class="step-number">Reference</span>
    <span class="step-title">What Cannot Go Into a Public AI Tool</span>
  </div>
  <div class="step-rule"></div>
  <p style="font-size:13px;margin-bottom:12px;">Unless you have a compliant enterprise agreement with the vendor that explicitly covers this data:</p>
  <ul class="checklist">
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>Client names, contact information, or account details</li>
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>Employee personal information</li>
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>Financial records, pricing, or budget details</li>
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>Anything covered by a client confidentiality agreement or NDA</li>
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>Health information of any kind</li>
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>Proprietary processes, unreleased product plans, or trade secrets</li>
  </ul>
  <p style="font-size:13px;color:#666;margin-top:12px;font-style:italic;">When in doubt: if you wouldn't attach it to an email and send it to someone outside the organisation, don't put it into a public AI tool.</p>
</div>
