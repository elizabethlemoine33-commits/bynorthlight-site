<h1 class="doc-title">Is Your AI Context-Ready?</h1>
<p class="doc-subtitle">Score your organisation across five context layers — identify what your AI can't see before upgrading tools or expanding rollout</p>

<div class="step">
  <div class="step-header">
    <span class="step-number">Layer 1</span>
    <span class="step-title">Conversational Context — The "Why"</span>
  </div>
  <div class="step-rule"></div>
  <ul class="checklist">
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>Your AI can access relevant conversation history — not just structured data</li>
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>Key decisions made in meetings, Slack, or email are captured somewhere searchable</li>
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>Your AI understands the reasoning behind past decisions, not just the outcomes</li>
  </ul>
</div>

<div class="step">
  <div class="step-header">
    <span class="step-number">Layer 2</span>
    <span class="step-title">Structured Data — The "What"</span>
  </div>
  <div class="step-rule"></div>
  <ul class="checklist">
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>Your AI is connected to your core business systems (CRM, project management, finance tools)</li>
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>It can distinguish between current and stale data</li>
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>Critical business data is not siloed in systems the AI cannot access</li>
  </ul>
</div>

<div class="step">
  <div class="step-header">
    <span class="step-number">Layer 3</span>
    <span class="step-title">Process Context — The "Action"</span>
  </div>
  <div class="step-rule"></div>
  <ul class="checklist">
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>Your AI understands how work moves through the organisation — approvals, handoffs, status changes</li>
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>Key processes are documented in a way the AI can parse, not just in people's heads</li>
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>Exceptions and informal workarounds are captured, not assumed away</li>
  </ul>
</div>

<div class="step">
  <div class="step-header">
    <span class="step-number">Layer 4</span>
    <span class="step-title">Knowledge &amp; Files — The "How"</span>
  </div>
  <div class="step-rule"></div>
  <ul class="checklist">
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>Your AI has access to relevant documentation, playbooks, and institutional knowledge</li>
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>That knowledge is current — it's not working from outdated wikis or stale onboarding guides</li>
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>Expertise that lives in people's heads has been captured in a form the AI can access</li>
  </ul>
</div>

<div class="step">
  <div class="step-header">
    <span class="step-number">Layer 5</span>
    <span class="step-title">Organisational Context — The "Who"</span>
  </div>
  <div class="step-rule"></div>
  <ul class="checklist">
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>Your AI knows who owns what decisions in your organisation</li>
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>It can route work to the right person — it doesn't treat the org as a flat list of names</li>
    <li><div class="check-box"></div>Role changes, reporting lines, and decision authority are kept up to date</li>
  </ul>
</div>

<div class="step">
  <div class="step-header">
    <span class="step-number">Score</span>
    <span class="step-title">What your results mean</span>
  </div>
  <div class="step-rule"></div>
  <ul class="checklist">
    <li><div class="check-box"></div><strong>0–2 layers covered:</strong> Your AI is likely frustrating your team more than helping. Before buying new tools, close the context gaps.</li>
    <li><div class="check-box"></div><strong>3–4 layers covered:</strong> Real progress — but the gaps you have are costing you. Prioritise the weakest layer and build from there.</li>
    <li><div class="check-box"></div><strong>5 layers covered:</strong> You're positioned to scale AI effectively. Most organisations aren't here yet.</li>
  </ul>
</div>
