## What Northlight Advisory Services is

<div class="answer-block"><p>Northlight Advisory Services is a Halifax, Nova Scotia–based strategy, AI adoption, and fractional COO advisory practice founded by Elizabeth Lemoine. It serves Canadian founders and operators at organisations typically under 50 people — remote-first, Canadian market primary. The work is decision clarity: helping leaders figure out what to do and how to do it, not producing strategy documents or frameworks.</p></div>

Northlight Advisory Services is a Halifax, Nova Scotia–based advisory practice founded by Elizabeth Lemoine. It is remote-first and serves primarily Canadian clients — founders, operations heads, and business leaders at small-to-mid-sized organisations (typically under 50 people) navigating growth, change, or technology adoption.

The tagline is: **Clear thinking. Real results.**

That's not a mission statement — it's a description of the work. The business exists to help leaders think more clearly and make better decisions. Not to produce strategy documents. Not to recommend frameworks. To help the people running organisations figure out what to do and how to do it.

## What we do

<div class="answer-block"><p>Four services: strategy advisory (decision clarity, not deck production); fractional COO engagements (director-level operations and strategy capacity without a full-time hire, structured as monthly retainers); practical AI adoption guidance grounded in Canadian regulatory context (PIPEDA, OPC guidance, provincial privacy law); and purpose-built tools — Northlight Vault (free Windows file browser) and Northlight Skills (MCP skills for Claude on MCP Market).</p></div>

### Strategy advisory

Northlight Advisory Services helps organisations identify what actually matters, set clear direction, and build plans that hold up under pressure. This is not strategy document production — it is decision clarity. The output is a leader who knows what to do next, not a slide deck about what they should consider doing.

### Fractional COO advisory

Northlight Advisory Services provides ongoing thinking partnerships for founders and operators, including fractional COO engagements where Elizabeth Lemoine works as an embedded part of a team for a defined period. This is director-level operations and strategy capacity without a full-time hire. Engagements are structured as monthly retainers with defined hours and clear scope.

### AI adoption for Canadian businesses

Northlight Advisory Services provides practical AI implementation guidance for Canadian businesses. This means identifying which workflows AI actually improves, which tools fit the business's regulatory context, and what governance is needed. The work is grounded in Canadian context: [PIPEDA](https://www.priv.gc.ca/en/privacy-topics/privacy-laws-in-canada/the-personal-information-protection-and-electronic-documents-act-pipeda/){:rel="noopener"}, the OPC's ChatGPT investigation findings, and provincial privacy requirements. It is not AI evangelism — it is AI implementation with clear eyes about both the benefits and the constraints.

### Apps and tools

Northlight Advisory Services builds small, purposeful tools that solve real workflow problems. [Northlight Vault](/vault.html) is a free Windows desktop application that lets users browse files across Google Drive, OneDrive, and Dropbox in a single interface. [Northlight Skills](https://mcpmarket.com/sellers/northlight-skills){:rel="noopener"} is a collection of purpose-built MCP skills for Claude, available on MCP Market. App development is also offered as a client service when the problem is specific enough to deserve a real solution.

## What Northlight Advisory Services is not
