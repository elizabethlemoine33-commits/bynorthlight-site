What it does

Northlight Vault is a read-only Windows desktop application that displays files from Google Drive, OneDrive, and Dropbox in one unified view — without uploading, editing, or moving anything.

🗂️

One view, three services

Google Drive, OneDrive, and Dropbox — all visible in a single interface. Browse folders, find files, and open them without switching apps or accounts.

🔒

Read-only by design

Vault is a browser, not an editor. It finds and opens your files — it doesn't move, rename, or delete anything. Your files stay exactly where they are.

🖥️

Desktop, not browser

A proper Windows desktop application. Open it from your taskbar. No browser tab to lose, no sign-in prompt every session. It's just there when you need it.
