How it works

01

Connect your accounts

Link your Google Drive, Microsoft OneDrive, and Dropbox accounts in seconds. No new account required — Vault connects to storage you already have.

02

Browse everything at once

See all your files across every connected provider in a single interface. No more remembering which platform something is on.

03

Open files instantly

Click any file and it opens in its native app. Fast, simple, and always in the right place.

Good to know

Vault is a read-only file browser — it lets you find and open files, not move, rename, or delete them.

It's not a cloud storage service and doesn't copy your files anywhere. Everything stays where it already is.

Northlight Vault does not collect or store your personal data — everything stays on your device. Read the Northlight Vault Privacy Policy →
