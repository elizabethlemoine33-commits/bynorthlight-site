Common Questions

How does a Northlight engagement start?
Start with a Clarity Call — a $99 focused session where we define your challenge and explore whether a Northlight engagement is the right fit. Email support@bynorthlight.ca with a paragraph describing the problem. Elizabeth reads every inquiry personally and responds within one business day.
What does advisory cost?
Advisory engagements are bespoke — structured around your specific situation and scope. A Clarity Call is $99 and is the right starting point for understanding what an engagement would look like and what it would cost. There are no packages because the work doesn't come in packages.
Do you work with businesses outside Canada?
Occasionally. Northlight Advisory Services focuses on Canadian organisations because they face a distinct set of regulatory, funding, and market conditions — particularly around AI adoption and privacy law (PIPEDA, Quebec Law 25) — that general advice doesn't address well.
Is the Clarity Call fee refundable?
The Clarity Call fee is non-refundable but credited toward an engagement if you move forward within 30 days.
