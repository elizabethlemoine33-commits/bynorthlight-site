Who I am

I'm Elizabeth Lemoine, founder of Northlight Advisory Services in Halifax, Nova Scotia. I first wrote about leadership and organizational behaviour in 1999, in a chapter of a community handbook, and I continue to write essays on strategy, AI adoption, and more on the blog, on LinkedIn, and in the Aurora Brief.

I work across strategy, software adoption, operations, and applied AI, because those four things are almost always connected to the same problem.

“Most organisations aren't drowning in problems. They're drowning in information and starving for direction.”

I look at the whole picture, without an agenda, and work out what's going on, what matters, and what you do next. That might be a strategy decision, an operational redesign, an AI adoption plan, or a tool we build together. Most engagements touch more than one.

I build operations around Canadian privacy requirements: PIPEDA federally, Quebec's Law 25, and the private-sector privacy acts in Alberta and British Columbia.

Northlight Advisory Services exists so (new) CEOs and founders have the support and resources they need to grow their ideas into the products and services that actually change things for the better.

If you're one of the founders who should have that support and those resources, I'd genuinely like to hear what you're building.

Questions

What does Northlight Advisory Services do?

It helps Canadian founders and small leadership teams make clearer decisions, run better operations, use AI well, and handle Canadian privacy requirements. Most clients work with Elizabeth through Embedded Advisory (about 20 hours a month) or On-Call Advisory (up to 5 hours a month for a flat monthly rate).

Who does Elizabeth work with?

Primarily Canadian founders, directors, and operations leaders at small to mid-sized organisations, typically under 50 people, who have an operational challenge and need clear thinking they can act on quickly.

How does an engagement start?

With a free 30-minute Coffee Chat, then a second conversation, then a plan we both agree on before any work begins.

What is fractional leadership?

Bringing in a senior operator, here Elizabeth, as an embedded part of your team for a defined period, without hiring a full-time executive. It suits organisations that need director-level operations or strategy capacity now but aren't ready to hire permanently.

Does Northlight Advisory Services build tools as well as advise?

Yes. Elizabeth has built and uses three production tools, Northlight Vault, Northlight Meetings, and Northlight OS, and can act as the go-between for your team and a developer.

Where is Northlight Advisory Services based?

Founded and led by Elizabeth Lemoine in Halifax, Nova Scotia. Engagements are remote, with a focus on Canadian organisations, though geography is negotiable when the problem is specific.

Can you help with operations and an AI roadmap at the same time?

Yes. Most engagements touch more than one area. Fixing how work gets done and deciding where AI fits are usually the same conversation, so I treat them as one plan.

Do you work with businesses outside Halifax?

Yes. Engagements are remote, so location does not limit who I work with. I work with Canadian organisations across the country, and I am based in Halifax.
