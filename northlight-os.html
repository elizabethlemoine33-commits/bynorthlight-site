The problem

Running a solo advisory and app development practice means constant context-switching. Tasks live in ClickUp. Client emails arrive in Gmail. Finances are scattered across bank statements and email receipts. Content ideas sit in Slack. No off-the-shelf tool connects all of that in a way that actually matches how a one-person practice works.

The options were: pay for five separate tools and check them all manually, or build the right tool. Northlight OS is the right tool.

System architecture

AI skills framework

Northlight OS runs 18+ reusable Claude skills — modular agents that can be chained together or run independently. Tasks in ClickUp are assigned to a skill, and the system handles the rest.

Content Strategist
Defines messaging, audience, and content structure before any writing begins
Draft
Writes first-pass content from a brief — articles, posts, reports, emails
Critic
Reviews a draft against brand voice, strategy, and audience fit — returns scored feedback
Polish
Copy-edits and refines writing for clarity, tone, and flow
Research
Compiles structured research briefs from web sources and Drive files
Expense Extract
Parses expense emails to extract vendor, amount, and date reliably
Revenue Extract
Parses payment confirmation emails to extract client and amount
Weekly Review
Generates a structured week-in-review summary from ClickUp data
Draft → Critic → Polish
Full content pipeline in one skill chain — strategist, draft, critique, and final polish
