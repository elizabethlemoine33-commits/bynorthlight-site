Northlight Advisory Is Featured in Digital Reference as One of the Best Fractional COO Services in Canada

We are incredibly proud to announce that Northlight Advisory and our founder, Elizabeth Lemoine, have been officially recognized by Digital Reference on the definitive list of the Best Fractional COO Services in Canada.

This editorial milestone highlights our dedicated focus on helping modern businesses build stronger operations, align leadership teams, and deploy highly scalable business systems. Inspired by the guiding utility of the Northern Lights, Northlight Advisory acts as a trusted, high-impact operational compass for organizations seeking to navigate growth, transition, and technical evolution with confidence.

Who We Serve and How We Work

Northlight Advisory operates as a business operations consultancy and fractional executive practice built specifically for founder-led businesses, growing small-to-mid-sized companies (SMBs), and ambitious leadership teams throughout Canada. Our clients are typically scaling organizations that have reached an operational tipping point — they need director-level systems design, governance frameworks, and strategic operational oversight, but do not yet require the overhead of a full-time, permanent Chief Operating Officer (COO).

Our operating model replaces high-level, detached strategies with direct, senior-led, hands-on execution. When you partner with Northlight Advisory, you do not get passed off to junior associates or handed a hundred-page slide deck. Instead, we embed directly into your executive structure — designing integrated human resources and financial systems, establishing international compliance frameworks, and mapping out clear team accountability protocols.

The Role of Fractional COO Consultants Today

In today's business landscape, relying on fragmented legacy applications, undocumented workflows, or manual habits presents real commercial risk. When a scaling business experiences a lack of operational oversight, leadership misalignment, or poor internal systems, product launches stall, team morale drops, and cash flow suffers. This is why forward-thinking founders and growing companies collaborate with specialized fractional COO advisors to build sound operating models, optimize their digital infrastructure, and streamline daily workflows.

Partnering with a fractional COO practice delivers market advantage by prioritizing:

  • Fractional COO Leadership: Embedding an experienced, part-time Chief Operating Officer directly into your business to guide systems, governance, and team coordination.
  • Strategic Operational Planning & Auditing: Running comprehensive deep-dives on your current operational structures to clarify priorities and remove growth bottlenecks.
  • Systems Design & Process Automation: Standardizing workflows across key platforms — such as Salesforce CRM or ClickUp — to reduce manual errors and improve team output quality.
  • Canadian-First Regulatory Compliance: Structuring AI adoption and corporate operations within Canadian regulatory frameworks, including PIPEDA and OPC guidelines.
  • Leadership Alignment & Governance: Developing unified operational manuals, board policies, and cross-departmental KPIs to ensure teams move in the same direction.

Meet Our Founder: Elizabeth Anne Lemoine

Northlight Advisory was established to provide scaling Canadian businesses with a direct, highly practical, and technically sophisticated operational partner — converting daily chaos into a clean, automated engine of growth.

As the Founder and Fractional COO of Northlight Advisory, Elizabeth Anne Lemoine brings over a decade of director-level operations leadership across the technology, non-profit, and global enterprise sectors. Throughout her career, she has managed cross-functional operations across 100+ countries, steered 300+ major strategic initiatives, and designed complex global compliance frameworks from the ground up. Elizabeth specializes in making modern business solutions — including AI adoption — practical and secure rather than purely academic.

Elizabeth's leadership style is characterized by direct candor, clear accountability frameworks, and a relentless focus on practical execution. Known for her ability to sit down with visionary founders and rapidly distill their goals into realistic, step-by-step technical roadmaps, she bridges the gap between executive vision and day-to-day work.
