Who is Northlight Advisory Services

Northlight Advisory Services is a Halifax, Nova Scotia–based fractional COO and AI adoption advisory practice for Canadian small businesses. It's built for founder-led organisations typically under 50 people that are navigating a strategic or operational inflection point. One person, accountable end-to-end, not a team of junior staff billing by the hour.

What we do

  1. Fractional COO & Advisory

    Ongoing thinking partnership for founders and operators who need a sharp outside perspective, and someone who can actually run things. A fractional COO becomes an embedded part of your leadership team, driving operations, process design, and execution. Where the work requires it, that scope extends to CIO-level: AI strategy, knowledge systems, technology governance. A relationship that moves the work forward, month over month.

  2. Strategy

    Clarity before execution. Northlight Advisory Services helps organisations diagnose what's actually blocking progress, and build a path forward that holds up when the plan meets reality.

  3. AI Adoption

    Practical AI implementation for Canadian businesses: what applies to your operations, not what's trending. Northlight Advisory Services identifies the tasks AI is actually good for and builds from there.
