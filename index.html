What we do

Northlight Advisory Services provides strategy, fractional COO and CIO advisory, AI adoption, and purpose-built app development to small and mid-sized organisations in Halifax, Nova Scotia and across Atlantic Canada.

Strategy

Clarity before execution. Northlight Advisory Services helps organisations diagnose what's actually blocking progress — and build a path forward that holds up when the plan meets reality.

Fractional COO & Advisory

Ongoing thinking partnership for founders and operators who need a sharp outside perspective — and someone who can actually run things. Fractional COO engagements bring Elizabeth in as an embedded part of your leadership team to drive operations, process design, and execution. Where the work requires it, engagements can also extend to CIO-scope: AI strategy, knowledge systems, and technology governance. Not a retainer that fills a calendar. A relationship that moves the work.

AI Adoption

Practical AI implementation for Canadian businesses — not what's trending, but what applies to your operations. Northlight Advisory Services identifies the tasks AI is actually good for and builds from there.

Apps

Small, purposeful tools built to solve real workflow problems. Northlight Vault is one. Northlight Advisory Services builds for clients too, when the problem is specific enough to deserve a real solution.

Northlight Meetings is a Windows desktop app that records client calls, transcribes them using Groq Whisper, and runs AI analysis to extract action items, key decisions, and follow-up dates. It then pushes structured notes to Google Drive, tasks to ClickUp, and events to Google Calendar — automatically. Coming soon.
Northlight Vault is a free Windows desktop app that lets you browse files across Google Drive, OneDrive, and Dropbox in a single interface — without switching tabs or apps. It's a read-only file browser: it finds and opens files, it doesn't move or delete them. Free.
